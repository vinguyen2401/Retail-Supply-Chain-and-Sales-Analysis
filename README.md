# Retail Supply Chain and Sales Analysis

This project is part of the **FP20 Analytics Challenge**, undertaken to refine my data analysis and visualization skills, with a specific focus on evaluating performance metrics across various product categories.

## Project Background
The analysis focuses on the performance of different product categories in retail logistics, specifically targeting delivery efficiency, return rates, and profitability. The project aims to uncover category-specific insights to help create a logistics strategy that not only improves operational efficiency but also aligns with customer expectations for timely and cost-effective deliveries.

## Objective
- **City/State Retail Supply Chain Management by Category:** Identify which cities or states excel in managing supply chains for specific product categories.
- **Average Delivery Days by Product Category:** Analyze the delivery time performance across categories such as furniture, office supplies, and technology.
- **Category-wise Segment Performance:** Examine delivery efficiency within each product category across different customer segments.
- **Fastest Delivered Products by Category:** Identify the top 5 fastest-delivered products within each category.
- **Slowest Delivered Products by Category:** Highlight the 5 slowest-delivered products within each category.
- **Category-specific Return Rates:** Evaluate which product categories experience the lowest and highest return rates.
- **Top Profitable Customers by Product Category:** Identify the top 5 most profitable customers for each product category.

## Insights Deep Dive by Category

### Best Supply Chain Performance by Category:
- **Furniture:** West Virginia has the best supply chain management for furniture, with an average delivery time of 3 days.
- **Office Supplies:** New York performs best, with an average of 4 days for deliveries.
- **Technology:** California leads with a fast delivery time of 5 days compared to the national average.

### Average Delivery Days by Category:
- **Furniture:** The average delivery time for furniture is 34.37 days, indicating a potential area for improvement.
- **Office Supplies:** The average is slightly higher at 34.55 days, requiring a focus on streamlining this category’s logistics.
- **Technology:** Delivery times average 35.08 days, suggesting that faster alternatives could improve customer satisfaction.

### Segment-wise Category Performance:
- **Furniture:**
  - **Consumer:** Average delivery time is 33.8 days.
  - **Corporate:** Longer at 35.2 days, signaling delays for business customers.
- **Office Supplies:**
  - **Home Office:** 34.4 days on average, performing slightly better than other segments.
- **Technology:**
  - **Corporate:** Averages 35.5 days, the slowest among segments.

### Fastest Delivered Products by Category:
- **Furniture:** The fastest product is the Anker USB Car Charger.
- **Office Supplies:** Avery 482 stands out with swift delivery.
- **Technology:** Cisco IP Phone leads in delivery speed.

### Slowest Delivered Products by Category:
- **Furniture:** HP D4360 Printer is the slowest to be delivered.
- **Office Supplies:** The Xerox Blank Paper set faces the most significant delays.
- **Technology:** Vtech Speakerphone has one of the longest delivery times in this category.

### Category-specific Return Rates:
- **Furniture:** Experiences a moderate return rate of 7.5%, with potential improvement areas in packaging and handling.
- **Office Supplies:** Shows the lowest return rate at 4.3%, indicating customer satisfaction with this category.
- **Technology:** The highest return rate of 10.2%, possibly linked to product quality or technical issues.

### Top 5 Profitable Customers by Category:
- **Furniture:** Tamara Chand leads with $4.5k in profit.
- **Office Supplies:** Raymond Buch contributes $3.2k in profits.
- **Technology:** Sanjit Chand tops the list with $5.8k in profit.

## Key Recommendations
1. **Improve Technology Category Delivery Efficiency:** Focus on shortening delivery times for technology products by partnering with specialized carriers and optimizing warehouse operations.
2. **Reduce Return Rates for Technology Products:** Analyze the reasons behind the high return rate in this category, possibly due to product defects or complex technical issues. Addressing these will reduce returns and improve customer loyalty.
3. **Streamline Office Supplies Deliveries:** Leverage the current low return rates in office supplies to improve delivery speed by reviewing supplier and carrier contracts, ensuring faster processing for this category.
